How it works
  1. Get the problem.Open-ended, built on real data, with no single right answer.
  2. Build a model with your team.Math, code, spreadsheets: whatever works. Papers are due mid-afternoon.
  3. Defend it.Finalists present to judges; awards and raffles close the night.
AI policy. Use AI to find models and write code. Judges score how well you can justify every decision.

Sample problem
Not this year’s

Northern Virginia is the world’s largest data-center market, and Dominion Energy projects its Virginia electricity load could roughly double within 15 years. (1) Model a data center’s power draw across a year. (2) Forecast the region’s demand 5–15 years out. Will it outgrow supply? Quantify your uncertainty. (3) Recommend where the next ones should go.

Data provided: EIA Form 861 · PJM load forecasts · Dominion’s Integrated Resource Plan · JLARC data-center study (2024) · Loudoun County GeoHub. See a winning paper: TJ’s 2023 M3 Challenge champion.
