What is TJM²

TJM² is a free, one-day math modeling competition run by TJ students. Teams of three to five get an open-ended, real-world problem in the morning, build a model from real data, and write it up by afternoon; the strongest teams then present and defend their work. Our first edition aims to bring 150+ middle and high schoolers, with separate divisions and $2,000+ in prizes.

Who we are

The TJ Math Modeling Club turns messy, real-world questions into math. Our teams won the MathWorks Math Modeling (M3) Challenge in 2023, first of about 650 teams, placed second in 2024, and reached the top six in 2026. Our members have also reached national olympiad camps (MOP and USACO Camp) and done research at MIT PRIMES and RSI.

Why we need you

TJM² is entirely student-run, with no FCPS or school funding behind it. Every dollar goes to participants: food, prizes, T-shirts, printing, and the custodial staff who open the building on a Saturday. Sponsors keep it free, which lets us reach students whose schools have no math team at all.

Applied math gave many of us our start. We want every student in Northern Virginia to get that chance, whatever school they attend and whatever their family can afford.
