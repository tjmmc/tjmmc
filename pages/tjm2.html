TJM²

The Math Modeling Hackathon, where teams apply mathematical models to analyze real-world issues.

what is it?

An 8-hour team hackathon. instead of building apps, you're creating mathematical models, M3-style. Teams research a real-world issue, develop an original model to address it, and submit a written paper outlining their approach, assumptions, and results, which is evaluated by a panel of experts, culminating with top teams presenting their model live.

why you should join.

  • A day out with friends. Bring your team, or find one there.
  • FREE FOOD. We will get pizza or Chipotle or boba.
  • $2500 in prizes. Just submit a paper and there will be prizes. Raffles throughout.
  • No experience needed. We'll present everything you need on the day-of. Come to 8th periods or our discord for extra prep and questions.

m3 recruitment.

Top performers are considered in our team selection for the international M3 challenge, where we've recently won $20,000 and $15,000.
