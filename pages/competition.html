Competitions

We participate in variety of competitions, such as...

M3 Math Modeling Challenge

TJ has a strong track record in the M3 Challenge, SIAM's annual math modeling competition sponsored by MathWorks. In 2023, a TJ team was named one of six overall finalists and one of three finalists for the Technical Computing Scholarship Award. In 2024, TJ won the competition and $20,000. Only Juniors and Seniors are able to compete.

HiMCM

TJ regularly participates in HiMCM (High School Mathematical Contest in Modeling), COMAP's international competition where teams of up to 4 students work within a two-week contest window. In 2024, a TJ team earned an Honorable Mention designation.

Modeling the Future Challenge

MTFC is a national scholarship competition, run by The Actuarial Foundation and the Institute of Competition Sciences, where high school teams of one to five students take on the role of an actuary, using math modeling and data analysis to assess real-world risk.
