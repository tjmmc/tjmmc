M² TJ

The Math Modeling Hackathon, where teams apply mathematical models to analyze real-world issues.

about.

A one-day competition hosted by TJHSST in which teams of 2–5 middle or high school students have 8 hours to research a real-world issue and develop an original mathematical model to address it. Teams submit a written paper outlining their approach, assumptions, and results, which is then evaluated by a panel of experts, culminating with top teams presenting their model live.
