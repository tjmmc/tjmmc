The problems

Pick one

Problem 1 · Data centers in Loudoun County

What will be the total energy consumption over the next 10 years of the data centers in Loudoun County?

Problem 2 · Spotted lanternflies

What will be the predicted damage to the tree ecosystem in Northern Virginia due to spotted lanternflies over the next 20 years?

The full problem statements and starter sources are in the packet.

What a strong paper does

  1. Defines the problem.Who’s affected, what data matters, and what each variable means.
  2. States and justifies its assumptions.The ones that make the problem solvable.
  3. Explains the model and why.The best-justified model wins, not the fanciest.
  4. Analyzes the results.Including a sensitivity analysis: does it hold at 5 or 50 years?
  5. Weighs strengths and weaknesses.What you’d improve and what you traded off.

Format

  • Three pages for the paper. The appendix doesn’t count: put your code, data and sources there.
  • Using an AI model? Include your prompts in the appendix.
  • Graphs and tables help. Judges love a clear visual.
  • See an example: a 2023 TJMMC paper.
