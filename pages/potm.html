Problem of the Month

This month’s problem

A modeling problem we (try to) update monthly. Send us your solution for practice writing papers.

The problem

How would you model an epidemiological outbreak’s economic effects on a secluded population? Imagine SARS hit a remote tribe. How would it affect trade and finances between communities? Describe how you would model parts of a regional economy using factors like individual economic participation, goods production, and whatever else you think matters. If you want to build a model and simulate it with parameters, include that too!
