Activities

What we do

Competitions, lectures, research and talks, plus our own competition this November.

TJM²

Our own one-day math modeling competition for middle and high schoolers, on Saturday, November 14, 2026. It’s free, there’s food and $2,000+ in prizes, and no experience is needed.

Competitions

We compete in the M3 Challenge, HiMCM, SIMIODE and the Modeling the Future Challenge.

The MathWorks Math Modeling (M3) Challenge is our flagship. Teams get 14 hours over Challenge weekend to write a solution paper on a real-world problem: building models, justifying assumptions, defining parameters and testing how the results change. The top six teams win scholarships and present in New York City. TJ teams won in 2023 ($20,000), placed second in 2024 ($15,000) and reached the finals again in 2026.

8th periods

Lectures on the techniques the contests need: regression, Markov chains, sensitivity analysis, paper writing, MATLAB and more. Browse the archive on the lectures page. Have an idea, or want to present? Pitch us at {{ links.email }}.

Research projects

Group or club-wide research projects, either academic or serving our community. We publish the papers, project reports and competition submissions ourselves to encourage mathematical exploration.

In-house contests

Internal contests that feed into team selection, often asking you to predict something a month out, so there’s suspense when the answer arrives. Plus our Problem of the Month. Compete for prizes and food.

Talks

Experts show how modeling shows up in their work, from rockets and weather to materials, medicine and finance, with time for Q&A. Talks can run during 8th period and stream to younger students at local middle schools, and speakers can go on to judge or mentor projects.
