Everything About TJM²

competitions.

We compete in the M3 Challenge, HiMCM, SIMIODE, and the Modeling the Future Challenge. The M3 Challenge is our flagship competition: a national program where teams get 14 hours over Challenge weekend to write a solution paper on a real-world problem. It involves building models, justifying assumptions, defining parameters, and analyzing effects of change. The top 6 teams receive tuition scholarships and present their papers to professionals in New York City. We've recently won the top prize ($20,000) and second place the year after ($15,000).

8th periods.

We give lectures on math modeling to prepare for the many contests, detailing MATLAB, regressions, paper-writing, and more. Browse the archive on the lectures page. Have an idea or want to present? Pitch us at {{ site.data.links.email }}.

research projects.

Group-wide or club-wide research projects, either academic or serving the nearby community. We self-publish the papers, project reports, and competition submissions to encourage mathematic exploration.

in-house contests.

Periodic internal contests that feed into team selection, often regarding predicting future a month out, so there's suspense when the answer arrives. Plus our problem of the month. Compete for prizes and FOOD.

talks.

We bring in experts to show how math modeling shows up in day-to-day jobs, whether it be in rockets, weather, materials, medicine, finance, and more, with Q&A. Talks can run during 8th period and stream to younger students at local middle schools. Each speaker can become a future judge or a project mentor.
