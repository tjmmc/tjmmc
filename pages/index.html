What we do

Math competitions are about clever tricks.
Modeling is about the real world.

We learn techniques most students never see in high school, like regression, Markov chains, Monte Carlo and differential equations, then use them on open-ended problems with real data, on a deadline, as a team.

The M3 Challenge

Our flagship contest: 14 hours to model a real problem from scratch. TJ teams won in 2023 and placed second in 2024.
